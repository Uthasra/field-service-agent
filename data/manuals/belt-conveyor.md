# Belt Conveyor — Service Manual (Model BC-1100)

## Section 2.1 Belt tracking
A belt that runs to one side is nearly always a frame squareness or idler
alignment problem, not a belt fault. Adjust one idler at a time, a quarter
turn, and run the belt for three full cycles before judging the change.

## Section 2.4 Belt wear limits
Replace belt BELT-1180 when the top cover is worn below 1.5 mm, when any edge
tear exceeds 50 mm, or when more than three splice fasteners have pulled out.

## Section 4.1 Roller replacement
Idler roller ROL-55 is replaced when it no longer spins freely by hand or when
a flat spot is visible. A seized roller abrades the belt cover quickly and is
the leading cause of premature belt replacement.

## Section 7.2 Lockout before any guard is opened
The conveyor must be locked out and tagged before any guard is opened. Guard
interlocks are a secondary protection and are never relied on as the primary
isolation.
