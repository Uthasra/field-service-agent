# Centrifugal Feedwater Pump — Service Manual (Model FP-50)

## Section 3.1 Vibration limits
Overall vibration velocity is measured at the bearing housing in mm/s RMS.
Below 4.5 mm/s the pump is in the acceptable band and no action is required.
Between 4.5 and 7.1 mm/s the pump is in the alert band: increase monitoring to
weekly and inspect the coupling alignment at the next opportunity.
Above 7.1 mm/s the pump must be shut down at the next safe stop. Continuing to
run above 11.0 mm/s risks bearing failure within days.

## Section 3.2 Common causes of high vibration
The four causes seen most often, in order of frequency, are shaft misalignment
after a coupling change, impeller imbalance from cavitation damage, worn
bearings, and a loose baseplate hold-down bolt. Misalignment shows a strong
2x running-speed component. Imbalance shows a dominant 1x component.
Bearing wear shows broadband high-frequency energy above 1 kHz.

## Section 4.2 Mechanical seal replacement
Isolate and lock out the pump, then drain the casing through the bottom plug.
Remove the coupling guard and the coupling spacer. Back off the four gland
nuts evenly. Withdraw the seal cartridge with the puller; never lever against
the shaft sleeve. Fit seal SEAL-208 dry, without grease on the elastomer
faces. Torque the gland nuts to 22 Nm in a cross pattern. Rotate the shaft by
hand for one full turn before restoring power.

## Section 4.5 Bearing replacement
Bearing 6206 is fitted at the drive end. Heat the bearing to 90 C in an
induction heater before fitting; never hammer the bearing onto the shaft.
Re-grease with 12 g of lithium complex grease. Do not overfill the housing;
more than one third full causes the bearing to run hot.

## Section 6.1 Cavitation
Cavitation is heard as gravel passing through the casing. It is caused by
suction pressure falling below the vapour pressure of the fluid. Check the
suction strainer for blockage first, then verify the supply tank level, then
confirm the suction valve is fully open. Prolonged cavitation erodes the
impeller and requires impeller IMP-4412 to be replaced.
